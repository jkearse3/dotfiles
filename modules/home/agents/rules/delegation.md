# Delegation

The user authorizes proactive delegation when its expected benefit from
independent judgment, specialization, parallel work, or context isolation
exceeds coordination and verification costs. Further delegation requires
explicit human authorization in the delegate's conversation.

Give each delegate a bounded scope, necessary context, expected result and
verification, and an explicit authority boundary no broader than your own. Do
not assign concurrent delegates overlapping writes; dispatch independent
assignments concurrently when supported.

Delegation does not bypass applicable skills or add authority. The caller
remains responsible for checking and integrating results.
